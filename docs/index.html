Joel Mokyr

Northwestern University

"If innovations are to succeed one another in a self-generating process, we not only need to know that something works, but we also need to have scientific explanations for why."

The Prescriptive Problem: Mokyr identified that sustained growth requires propositional knowledge—not just that a technique works, but why it works. This is precisely what computational models provide: explicit, inspectable, reproducible explanations.

Peter Howitt

Brown University

"Creative destruction creates conflicts that must be managed in a constructive manner. Otherwise, innovation will be blocked by established companies and interest groups that risk being put at a disadvantage."

The Prescriptive Problem: Their Schumpeterian growth model formalizes how innovation replaces old technologies. But the model is descriptive. We ask: given a set of agents, institutions, and technological possibilities, what mechanism design maximizes the rate of welfare-improving creative destruction?

The 2025 laureates showed us why creative destruction drives growth. Silmaril is an attempt to compute how to sustain it.

The Underlying Mathematics

Why Category Theory

The 2025 Nobel recognized that growth is not accidental—it requires specific institutional and epistemic preconditions. But economic theory, even at its most formal, typically operates at a single level of abstraction.

Category theory lets us relate multiple levels:

  • Lawvere theories encode institutional rules as algebraic theories, making the "rules of the game" explicit and composable
  • Sheaves model local economic states that glue together into global consistency—formalizing how local creative destruction aggregates
  • Bialgebras capture the duality between behavior and structure, between how agents act and what institutions constrain
  • Functorial semantics lets us interpret the same formal theory in different economic contexts—transferring insights across domains

This is not abstraction for its own sake. It is abstraction because the 2025 laureates showed us that the same mechanisms operate across history, across industries, across scales—and category theory is the mathematics of "the same."

Vol. 20

Functorial Transport

Ontology-to-schema movement is lawful only when it is functorial. An ontology is not a vocabulary list to be renamed into columns. It is an olog fragment: typed objects, typed arrows, path equations. A transport mapping F : C → D is valid only if it preserves identity and composition.

Core Machinery

  • Colimit preservation. The individual is not a taxon row or transported label. Mittens—the concrete cat—is defined by a cocone of typed legs. Distribute her into unrelated rows and the schema loses the compositional grammar that made the source meaningful.
  • Yoneda for transport. Use Hom-profiles to verify transported objects. A functor may preserve a colimit without reflecting it; isomorphism of profiles must imply isomorphism of apexes.
  • Cheese-trap immunity. Distinguish functors from raw data dumps. Source projection binds canonical origin keys to direct artifacts. Citation count does not equal source independence.
  • Pushforward vs. pullback. Information flow carries data; validation flow carries constraints. Both must be tracked separately.

Vol. 28

Evidence & Provenance

A claim may be promoted only when supported by three independent source legs. Not three copies of the same syndicated source—that collapses to one provenance fiber. True independence requires distinct origins verified by source projection. Promotion is a joint cocone. Downstream audit views must factor through the promoted claim apex.

The Six Certification Layers

  1. Existence. Mathematical existence (stars-and-bars, spectral theorem) does not yield an algorithm.
  2. Construction. A recipe does not guarantee runtime compatibility.
  3. Compatibility. Runtime checks ensure spectral tower, graph revision, and lambda term agree on dimension, revision, and type before a transition is admitted.
  4. Uniqueness. Game values are unique; equilibrium policies are not. Eigensystems require an orientation witness.
  5. Computation. Exact matrix games are not numerical approximations with positive tolerances. A positive saddle residual is not an exact certificate.
  6. Refusal. The system returns a typed Issue rather than manufacturing a fictitious result.

Lambda Blotto

The constitutive engine binding three domains as jointly constitutive:

  • Game theory: Discrete Colonel Blotto with hard-budget allocations and zero-sum payoffs
  • Spectral graph theory: Revision-bound eigenvalue/eigenvector modes via orthogonal diagonalization of graph Laplacians
  • Typed lambda calculus: Bounded evaluation over closed terms producing the transitions

Remove one domain and the Lambda-Blotto transition is undefined.
